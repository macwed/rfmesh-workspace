# WS-B Scratchpad — 2026-05-16

## WS-B-004 sigma path: ticket-prescribed polyfit-cov is not viable for Capon

### What was requested

The ticket prescribes (under "Sigma recipe"):
> SAME as WS-B-003: parabolic fit on `1/P_capon`, propagated variance via
> polyfit cov, empirically-calibrated multiplicative constant to make
> median(claimed) hit empirical_std at +/-5 % at SNR=20 dB.

i.e. fit `y = c2 x^2 + c1 x + c0` to `1/P` over the 7-point window around
the argmax; let `np.polyfit(..., cov=True)` return the chi-square-scaled
covariance of the coefficient estimates; propagate to `Var(x_v)` via the
Jacobian; multiply by a calibration constant `K`.

### What was tried

Implemented exactly as prescribed. Verified on the canonical scenario
(UCA N=4 at r=lambda/4, 137 deg, 4096 samples, default loading 1e-3) over
400-trial Monte Carlo at SNR={10, 20, 30} dB:

| SNR    | empirical std | median(sqrt(polyfit Var(x_v))) | implied K     |
|--------|---------------|---------------------------------|---------------|
| 10 dB  | 0.0958 deg    | 2.99e-5 deg                     | 3199          |
| 20 dB  | 0.0299 deg    | 2.73e-5 deg                     | 1093          |
| 30 dB  | 0.0094 deg    | 2.17e-5 deg                     | 433           |

Confirmed across fit-window sizes (7, 11, 15, 19 points) and across
seed offsets: the polyfit-cov sigma is essentially SNR-independent and
~5 orders of magnitude smaller than the empirical std.

### Why it does not work

The polyfit residual variance is the variance of the fit's residuals,
i.e. how far each `(x_i, y_i)` point lies from the fitted parabola. For
L1 (WS-B-001), the seven `(heading, RSSI)` samples each come from a
*fresh IQ block* with fresh thermal noise, so the residuals have honest
per-point statistical scatter and the polyfit cov scales correctly with
SNR. For Capon, all seven scan samples come from the *same* R; the
spectrum at each grid point is a deterministic function of one random
matrix. Residuals are dominated by the higher-order non-quadratic shape
of `1/P` (a deterministic systematic) and by FP round-off, not by
statistical noise -- so polyfit cov scales with the local non-quadratic
curvature, not with SNR.

This is a structural property of Capon, not a tuning issue. No single
constant `K` and no window-size choice can rescue it: the formula is
SNR-blind.

### What works instead: Fisher information from c0/c2

The Capon DoA asymptotic variance is

    Var(theta_hat) ~ K_capon * c0_unloaded / (2 * T * c2_unloaded)

where `c2_unloaded`, `c0_unloaded` are the curvature and value of the
parabolic fit to `1/P_capon` evaluated on the *lightly-loaded* R (loading
1e-6 of trace/N, just enough to guarantee invertibility), and `T` is the
snapshot count. This is the standard MVDR CRB-like estimator (the
curvature of `-log P` at the peak is the Fisher information per
snapshot; for a parabolic fit on `1/P`, `d^2 log(P)/dtheta^2 |peak = -2
c2 / c0`).

The peak location still uses the *heavily-loaded* R (loading 1e-3 by
default) because the peak position is the quantity the cond-number gate
protects; using two different loadings on the same R is a free
operation for N <= 8.

With this formula and `K_capon = 1.0`, the +/-20% honesty band is
comfortably met at all three test SNRs (median ratios 0.98, 0.97, 0.97).

### Decision

Implement the Fisher-information formula with split loading. Set
`K_capon = 1.0`. Document the deviation prominently in `l2_mvdr.py`'s
module docstring, link this scratchpad note, and call out the
structural reason (within-realization vs across-realization
residuals).

The ticket's spirit is *honest sigma*; the prescribed mechanism is
incompatible with that spirit for Capon, and the chosen mechanism
satisfies the actual acceptance gate (honesty test at +/-20% across
three SNRs).

A future contracts-friendly refactor could lift the Fisher formula into
a shared helper that MUSIC also uses; deferred to a follow-up ticket
after WS-B-003 lands.
